## RU-05 is why v2.3 exists

The acceptance test for "99% alive" was always CI-side. Every probe ran from a
GitHub Actions runner or an RU-datacenter bridge — never from the operator's
residential line through TSPU. v2.2's own investigation (phase 22.1) proved the
gap exists (95% Frankfurt vs 0% residential) and then measured against the wrong
side of it anyway.

Observed reality when the operator finally tested: 18 of 257 keys responded, and
all 18 were Shadowsocks — the protocol v2.2 had excluded as blocked.

## Carried into v2.3

- RU-05 restated so the operator's client is the measurement instrument.
- Protocol tiers re-derived from measured pass rates, not anecdote.
- The truncated speedtest (5s timeout ⇒ 16 Mbps ceiling, 54% of values are
  artifacts) that feeds `--min-speed 12` and silently discards good keys.
